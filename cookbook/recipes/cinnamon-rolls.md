# Cinnamon Rolls

## Ingredients

### Dough
- 240 ml warm milk, approx. 37 °C
- 7 g dry yeast
- 50 g sugar
- 10 g psyllium husk powder
- 360 g gluten-free flour blend for bread/yeast dough
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 egg, size M, room temperature
- 60 g soft butter

### Filling
- 60 g soft butter
- 80 g brown sugar
- 1.5 tbsp cinnamon

### Frosting
- 100 g cream cheese, full fat
- 80 g powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. **Activate the yeast** – Stir the yeast and 1 tbsp of the sugar into the warm milk. Stir in the psyllium husk and let it swell for 5 minutes.
2. **Knead the dough** – Mix the flour blend, remaining sugar and salt. Add the yeast mixture, egg and butter and knead for 3–4 minutes into a soft, slightly sticky dough.
3. **First rise** – Cover and let rise in a warm place for 60 minutes.
4. **Prepare the filling** – Mix butter, brown sugar and cinnamon into a spreadable paste.
5. **Roll out and fill** – Roll the dough out between two sheets of parchment paper to about 30 × 40 cm, spread the filling over it, leaving a 2 cm border along one long side.
6. **Roll up and cut** – Using the parchment paper, roll up tightly, cut into 12 pieces and place them in a greased baking dish (about 23 × 33 cm).
7. **Second rise** – Cover and let rise for 45 minutes. Preheat the oven to 180 °C (350 °F) top/bottom heat.
8. **Bake** – Bake for 20–25 minutes until golden brown, covering with aluminum foil after 15 minutes if needed.
9. **Frosting** – Let cool for 10 minutes. Beat cream cheese, powdered sugar and vanilla until smooth and spread over the warm rolls.

## Notes

- Check the flour blend, dry yeast, powdered sugar and vanilla extract for a "gluten-free" label or the crossed-grain symbol.
- If the flour blend already contains psyllium husk, use only 5 g of psyllium husk powder.
- If the dough is too sticky: chill for 30 minutes, then roll out.

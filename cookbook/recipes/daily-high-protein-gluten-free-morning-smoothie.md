# Daily High-Protein Gluten-Free Morning Smoothie

## Ingredients

- 300 ml cold water or unsweetened almond milk, adjust to desired thickness
- 250 g low-fat quark, 0.2% fat, primary protein base
- 60 g oats, certified gluten-free (crossed-grain symbol)
- 150 g banana, about 1 large, fresh or frozen
- 15 g whole flaxseeds, about 1.5 tbsp
- 50 g frozen mixed berries, unsweetened, 100% fruit

## Instructions

1. **Liquid first** – Pour the liquid into the blender container first so the dry oats and quark don't stick to the blades.
2. **Add the rest** – Add the quark, oats, banana, flaxseeds and frozen berries.
3. **Blend** – Blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth and emulsified.
4. **Serve** – Pour into glasses and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Equipment: high-speed blender, kitchen scale.
- Oats are the critical gluten point: only use oats that are certified gluten-free and carry the crossed-grain symbol. Regular oats are almost always cross-contaminated with wheat.
- If you use almond milk, check that it is unsweetened and has no added oat or barley components; with water the smoothie is also nut-free.
- Whole flaxseeds are only partly broken up in a normal blender; a high-speed blender works best, or use ground flaxseeds.
- Makes about 800 ml – two glasses, or one large serving.

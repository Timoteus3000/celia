# High-Protein Turkey Taco Bowl

## Ingredients

### Rice
- 520 g basmati rice, dry
- 1000 ml cold water
- 1 tsp salt

### Taco mix
- 1-2 tsp rapeseed or olive oil
- 1200 g lean turkey mince, raw, 3 packs of 400 g
- 520 g black beans, canned, drained weight, about 2 cans, rinsed thoroughly
- 1000 g chopped tomatoes, canned, about 2.5 cans of 400 g

### Spices
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. **Rinse the rice** – Rinse the rice under cold water until it runs clear.
2. **Boil the rice** – Put rice, 1000 ml cold water and 1 tsp salt into a pot and bring to a rolling boil on high (level 9).
3. **Simmer the rice** – As soon as it boils, reduce to low (level 2), cover with a tight-fitting lid and simmer undisturbed for 10–12 minutes.
4. **Rest the rice** – Remove from the heat and let rest covered for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork before weighing.
5. **Brown the turkey** – Heat the oil in a large pan or wok on high (level 7–8). Add the turkey mince and break it apart continuously with a spatula for 7–8 minutes until browned.
6. **Remove moisture** – If liquid pools at the bottom, tilt the pan and drain it, or boil it off on high so the dish doesn't get watery.
7. **Bloom the spices** – Sprinkle the spices directly onto the hot meat and stir for 30–60 seconds.
8. **Simmer the taco mix** – Add the black beans and chopped tomatoes. Reduce to medium (level 4–5) and simmer uncovered for 8–10 minutes until the sauce thickens.
9. **Portion – her containers (4×)** – Per container about 120 g cooked rice and 210–230 g taco mix.
10. **Portion – his containers (4×)** – Per container about 190 g cooked rice and 300–320 g taco mix.
11. **Cool and store** – Let the containers cool uncovered for 15–20 minutes, then seal with airtight lids. Keep refrigerated for 3–4 days, or freeze extra portions.
12. **Reheat** – Sprinkle 1–2 tsp water over the rice, place the lid loosely on top and microwave for 2.5–3 minutes.

## Notes

- Equipment: large frying pan or wok, large pot with tight lid, colander, 8 meal prep containers (about 1.0–1.2 l).
- Covers 2 full days of lunch and dinner for two people.
- Stove levels refer to a 1–9 scale.
- Gluten check – turkey mince: read the ingredient list. It should contain only turkey meat, salt and spices – no wheat starch, breadcrumbs or other gluten additives.
- Gluten check – spices: use single spices, not pre-mixed taco or fajita seasoning packets, which often contain wheat flour as a filler. Single spices can still be cross-contaminated; brands labeled gluten-free are safest.
- Canned beans and tomatoes are normally gluten-free, but check that the ingredient list contains nothing beyond beans/tomatoes, water, salt and acidifiers.
